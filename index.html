// why the silence happens

Every listing wants experience. Nobody wants to give it first.

Two hundred applications land on one junior opening. Recruiters can't read all of them, so they scan for something that looks different from the pile. A degree doesn't do that anymore. A certificate barely does either.

What actually gets noticed is a project you can talk about in specific, unrehearsed detail — the bug that took two days, the design you rebuilt halfway through, the moment it finally worked. That's not luck. That's what live, mentor guided building produces, and it's the whole idea behind every program below.

The resume filterSame degree, same skills list, same silence. Differentiation beats decoration.
The experience trapYou don't need a paycheck to prove capability. You need something you built and can defend.
The interview tellVague answers signal borrowed knowledge. Specific ones signal ownership.
